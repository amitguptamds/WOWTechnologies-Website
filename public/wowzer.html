The Vision

The Future of Accounting is Automated

Accountants and bookkeepers spend thousands of hours every year on tasks that machines can handle better, faster, and with fewer errors — data migration, backup management, reconciliation, and document processing. WOWzer Technologies exists to change that. We’re an AI-first technology company on a mission to eliminate manual, repetitive work from accounting workflows for good.

Our philosophy is simple: “If it has to be repeated, it can be automated.” Every product we build starts with a real pain point experienced by real accountants and bookkeepers — then we engineer a solution that removes the friction entirely.

Our current products — WOW Backup & Restore and WOW BookSwitch — are the foundation of a broader platform vision. These aren’t proof-of-concepts. They’re production-grade tools already serving accountants and bookkeepers across five countries.

Looking ahead, our roadmap includes AI-driven financial reporting, automated document processing, and intelligent workflow automation. The vision is aspirational, but it’s grounded in working products already in market — and a team that understands both the technology and the accounting profession from the inside out.

WOW Backup & Restore

Secure Backup and Recovery for Xero Organizations

Built for Xero Accountants and Bookkeepers. By Accountants.

WOW Backup & Restore isn’t generic cloud backup retrofitted for accounting and bookkeeping. It’s a purpose-built backup and recovery solution engineered specifically for Xero — designed to protect your clients’ financial data with the depth, compliance, and reliability that accountants and bookkeepers demand.

True API Restore

Restore to a new Xero organization via API in just a few clicks. Most competitors only export files for manual re-import. WOW actually restores using the Xero API — zero manual work required.

All Attachments Included

Every attachment is backed up in the base price. Competitors charge extra — or don’t back them up at all. Our unique deep-linking tool lets you browse and review any transaction and its attachment directly.

Data Residency Compliance

Data stored in your own AWS region — US, Canada, or Australia. Meet data sovereignty requirements and address the cyber liability insurance gap: insurers require 3 independent data sources, and Xero only provides 2.

7-Day Rolling Backup

Automated daily backups ensure you always have a recent, clean snapshot of every Xero organization.

Vault Storage

Long-term archival for inactive organizations — keep historical data accessible and protected.

Full Restore Service

Complete recovery to a new Xero org with no manual intervention — the industry’s most comprehensive restore.

Available now in the Xero App Store across the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and the United Kingdom.

WOW BookSwitch

QuickBooks Desktop to Xero Migration — Fast, Accurate & Fully Automated

Built by Accountants Who’ve Felt the Pain of Migration

Unlimited Conversions
